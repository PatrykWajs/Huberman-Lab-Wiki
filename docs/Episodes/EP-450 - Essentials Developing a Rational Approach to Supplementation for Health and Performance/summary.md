## Key Findings

- Behavioral tools (like light exposure and exercise) and nutrition should be the foundation of health before considering supplementation. — *[00:00:44](transcript.md#000044-slug)*
- Supplements cannot compensate for poor nutrition and should only be layered on top of healthy lifestyle habits. — *[00:01:05](transcript.md#000105-slug)*
- Foundational supplements (broad-spectrum vitamins/minerals, digestive enzymes, probiotics) can provide nutritional “insurance,” especially for those with limited dietary variety or increased activity. — *[00:05:07](transcript.md#000507-slug)*
- To choose supplements, assess sleep quality, nutrition, and budget before targeting specific outcomes like sleep, focus, or hormones. — *[00:06:29](transcript.md#000629-slug)*
- When addressing poor sleep, start with behavioral and nutritional strategies (caffeine timing, pre-bed feeding) before supplements. — *[00:09:02](transcript.md#000902-slug)*
- Magnesium threonate/bisglycinate and apigenin can help with sleep onset; myo-inositol may help with middle-of-the-night awakenings. Trial single ingredients first. — *[00:10:27](transcript.md#001027-slug)*
- Theanine can help sleep onset but may cause vivid dreams or nighttime awakenings for some; avoid if that’s a problem. — *[00:09:50](transcript.md#000950-slug)*
- Melatonin is not recommended due to excessive dosing in supplements and potential disruption of other biological systems. — *[00:13:40](transcript.md#001340-slug)*
- Hormone support supplements like Fadogia Agrestis and Tongkat Ali can increase testosterone/libido, but dosing and safety (cycling) are critical; individual response varies. — *[00:15:37](transcript.md#001537-slug)*
- Female hormone response to supplements may vary across the menstrual cycle; dosage and ingredient control are especially important for women. — *[00:19:19](transcript.md#001919-slug)*
- Cognitive enhancement supplements include stimulants (caffeine, yohimbine) and neuromodulators (alpha-GPC, L-tyrosine), which should be tried separately before combining. — *[00:20:26](transcript.md#002026-slug)*
- Omega-3 (EPA-rich) supplementation has broad benefits for mental, physical, and cognitive health; aim for >1g EPA per day. — *[00:26:53](transcript.md#002653-slug)*
- Food-mimic supplements (e.g., whey protein) can be useful but should not replace a significant portion of whole foods due to nutrient completeness and fiber. — *[00:29:47](transcript.md#002947-slug)*
- For all non-foundational purposes (sleep, hormone health, cognition), single-ingredient formulations are preferable to maximize control, effectiveness, and minimize adverse effects or costs. — *[00:31:03](transcript.md#003103-slug)*
- Supplements are potent tools but should support—not replace—behavioral and nutritional strategies in a broader health framework. — *[00:33:02](transcript.md#003302-slug)*

## Tools & Protocols

### Foundational Supplementation Protocol
- **What:** Daily use of a broad-spectrum vitamin/mineral supplement with digestive enzymes, probiotics, prebiotics, and adaptogens.
- **How:** Choose a product you can afford, ensure it matches your dietary needs, and take with food.
- **When:** Daily, especially if dietary variety is limited, activity is high, or following intermittent fasting.
- **Ref:** [00:05:07](transcript.md#000507-slug)

### Sleep Support Protocol
- **What:** Use magnesium threonate or bisglycinate and/or apigenin to promote sleep, myo-inositol for middle-of-the-night awakenings. Try theanine with caution.
- **How:** Start with one supplement alone for a week, assess results, then consider combinations if needed. Cease any supplement that causes adverse effects.
- **When:** 30–60 minutes before intended bedtime.
- **Ref:** [00:10:27](transcript.md#001027-slug)

### Hormone Augmentation Protocol (Testosterone/Libido)
- **What:** Fadogia Agrestis (600 mg/day; cycle 8w on/2w off or 12w on/1m off); Tongkat Ali (200-600 mg/day, does not require cycling).
- **How:** Only use within recommended dosage; monitor for side effects. Consider individual response and get blood work as needed.
- **When:** Daily, as indicated, and in cycles for Fadogia Agrestis.
- **Ref:** [00:15:37](transcript.md#001537-slug)

### Cognitive & Focus Protocol
- **What:** Caffeine (coffee, tea, or capsule—low dose); alpha-GPC (300-600 mg); L-tyrosine (500-1000 mg); optional stacking after single-ingredient trials.
- **How:** Begin with single agents, track subjective effects, combine only after optimally titrating individually.
- **When:** Prior to focused work or exercise, avoid use before bedtime.
- **Ref:** [00:20:26](transcript.md#002026-slug)

### Omega-3 Supplementation for General Health
- **What:** High-quality fish oil delivering 1–3 grams of EPA per day.
- **How:** Read labels to confirm EPA content (not just total omega-3s); consider budget and dietary needs.
- **When:** Daily, with preference for reaching >1g EPA/day.
- **Ref:** [00:26:53](transcript.md#002653-slug)

### Supplements / Compounds
- Vitamin and mineral broad-spectrum supplements (e.g., Athletic Greens, not product-endorsed)
- Digestive enzymes, probiotics, prebiotics, adaptogens
- Magnesium threonate, magnesium bisglycinate, myo-inositol, apigenin, L-theanine
- Melatonin (discouraged due to dose and safety)
- Fadogia Agrestis, Tongkat Ali, alpha-GPC, L-tyrosine, phenylethylamine
- Caffeine, yohimbine, alpha-yohimbine (rauwolscine)
- Omega-3 fatty acids, especially EPA-rich fish oil
- Whey protein, casein (milk protein), egg protein, plant proteins (including potato protein)
